Where the data comes from

Every profile comes from public information: the platforms' public data (such as the YouTube Data API and Twitch API) and the creator's own public pages and link-in-bio. We only list business emails that creators have published themselves.

  • Creators can ask to be removed at any time on the removal page, and we remove their profile.
  • Creators who want brand work can add themselves on the join page.
  • How we handle personal data is set out in our privacy notice.

Questions

What counts as a micro-creator here?

Anyone with roughly 2,000 to 100,000 followers on the platform we list them for. That is the range where creators still read their own inbox and brand deals are affordable.

Does every creator have an email?

No. Many creators do not publish one. Use the "has email" filter to see only those who do.

How do I get access after paying?

Whop confirms your trial or subscription, and your license key is emailed to the address you used at checkout. Paste it into CreatorScan and you are in.

Can I cancel?

Yes, from your Whop account, at any time. Your key stops working when the membership ends.

Can I email creators in bulk?

On Pro, yes: connect your own email account and CreatorScan sends each personalised email from it, up to 50 a day. Emails never go out from our servers' address, and each one includes a link creators can use to be removed from the directory.
