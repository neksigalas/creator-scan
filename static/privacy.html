Privacy notice

Last updated 14 September 2026

Who we are

CreatorScan is operated by DigitalDrop. For anything about your data, write to digitaldrophq@gmail.com.

This notice covers two groups of people

Creators whose public profiles appear in CreatorScan, and customers who buy access to it.

Creators: what we hold and where it comes from

For creators on YouTube, Twitch, TikTok and Instagram we hold information that is already public: channel or profile name and handle, public bio, profile links, follower count, public view and engagement figures, language and country where the platform shows them, and a business contact email only when the creator has published one on their profile or their own pages.

It comes from the platforms' public data (for example the YouTube Data API and the Twitch API) and from the creator's public profile pages and link-in-bio pages. We also calculate an engagement-based "authenticity score" from those public figures. It is an estimate, not a statement of fact about anyone.

Creators who add themselves on the join page give us the details they choose to enter.

Why we use it, and on what basis

We use creators' public business information so that brands can find creators to propose paid collaborations to. We rely on legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Creators who publish a business contact generally do so to receive exactly this kind of enquiry, and we limit what we hold to public, business-related information. For creators who join themselves, we rely on their request.

Your choice as a creator

You can object and ask us to delete your profile at any time using the removal form, or by email. We confirm the request with you and remove the profile. We then keep only a minimal record so the profile is not added back.

You also have the right to access, correct or restrict the data we hold about you.

Customers

When you buy CreatorScan, Whop processes the payment and sends us your email address, your plan and the status of your membership. We use them to create your license key, email it to you and switch access on or off. We do not see or store your card details. The searches, lists and exports you make are used only to run the service for you.

Customers must use creators' contact details lawfully, for relevant business proposals, and must honour any request from a creator to stop contacting them.

Who else handles the data

  • Supabase (database hosting)
  • Vercel (web hosting)
  • Whop (payments and memberships)
  • Google (the Gmail account we send license emails from)
  • Anthropic, only when a Pro customer uses the AI writer: the selected creator's public profile is sent to generate a draft email

Some of these providers process data outside the EU under their own contractual safeguards.

How long we keep it

We keep creator profiles while they are public and relevant, and update or delete them when a creator asks. We keep customer records for as long as the membership lasts, and afterwards for as long as tax and accounting rules require.

Complaints

If you think we have handled your data wrongly, please tell us first. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. In Greece that is the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (dpa.gr).
